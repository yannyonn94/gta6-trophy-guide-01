Roadmap to the platinum

Work the phases in order. Story first, because several trophies need a partner or a story moment that is only available while the story is running; collectibles and grinds last, because they are all doable in free roam after the story ends.

  1. Phase 1 — Story (18 trophies). Play the main story from Gone Fishing to the final mission. This earns every chapter trophy, the named story missions and the contact strands. Do not finish the final mission until Phase 2 is done.
  2. Phase 2 — Missables (5 trophies). Before the final mission, clear Hot Together, Retail Therapy, Catch Me If You Can, Actions Speak Louder and Bad Luck, I Guess. Each needs a partner, a choice, or a one-off moment — see the Missables tab. Keep a save from before any story choice.
  3. Phase 3 — Activities (13 trophies). Shooting Range gold medals, drone races, 20 different races, fishing against Cal, arcade games, stranger strands, the bowling alley and the Scooter Bros app. None of these is missable; work them in any order.
  4. Phase 4 — Collectibles & exploration (6 trophies). Meditation spots, collectible card sets, WAiNK at 100 locations, 100 vehicles and exploring all of Leonida. Sweep the map region by region.
  5. Phase 5 — Combat, heists & money (12 trophies). Combat Focus, bank vaults, armored trucks, gang operations, the jewelry store, stunts, challenges, laundering $250,000 of Marked Bills, spending $100,000 and buying every garage. These stack up naturally in free roam.
  6. Phase 6 — The platinum. Vice Got a Price! pops automatically once the other 54 are earned. The last trophy standing is usually A Long Road (100% Completion) — check the completion list in-game for what is left.

Phase counts come from the official descriptions in this guide's data file: 18 story, 5 choice/missable, 13 activities, 3 collectibles + 3 exploration, 7 combat + 5 grind, and the platinum itself — 55 in all.

Missable trophies — do these before the final mission

Five trophies depend on a partner being available, a story choice, or a one-off moment. Everything else in the list can be earned in free roam after the story ends.
